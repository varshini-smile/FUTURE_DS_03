# FUTURE_DS_03
Analyzed marketing funnel data to identify conversion drop-offs, channel performance, and opportunities to improve lead-to-customer conversion.

Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis

**Project Overview**

This project focuses on analyzing customer conversion performance using the Bank Marketing Dataset. The goal is to understand how customers move through the marketing funnel, identify drop-off points, evaluate the effectiveness of different marketing channels, and discover opportunities to improve conversion rates.

The analysis was performed using Excel for data cleaning and Power BI for interactive dashboard creation. The dashboard helps stakeholders monitor funnel performance, customer conversions, and channel effectiveness to make data-driven marketing decisions.


---

**Data Cleaning Process (Excel)**

The dataset was cleaned and prepared in Excel before importing into Power BI.

Steps Performed:

1. Imported the dataset into Excel.


2. Checked and removed duplicate records.


3. Verified column headers and data consistency.


4. Reviewed the dataset for missing or blank values.


5. Ensured numeric columns were properly formatted.


6. Created a Converted column:

Yes = 1

No = 0



7. Created an Engaged Lead column based on call duration:

Duration > 0 = 1

Duration = 0 = 0



8. Created an Age Group column for customer segmentation:

18–25

26–35

36–45

46–55

56+



9. Converted the data range into an Excel Table for easier analysis.


10. Saved the cleaned dataset .

**Business Questions & Answers**

1. Where are users dropping off in the funnel?

The funnel analysis shows that the largest drop-off occurs between the Engaged Lead stage and the Customer Conversion stage. Although many customers are contacted and engaged, only a smaller percentage ultimately subscribe to the offered banking product.


---

2. Which channels bring high-quality leads?

The Contact Method Analysis indicates that some communication channels generate significantly higher conversion rates than others. Channels with the highest customer conversion should be prioritized for future marketing campaigns as they bring higher-quality leads.


---

3. How can conversion rates be improved?

Conversion rates can be improved by:

Focusing on high-performing contact channels.

Increasing engagement with interested customers.

Optimizing campaign strategies based on historical performance.

Targeting customer segments with higher conversion potential.

Personalizing marketing communication.



---

4. Which stages need optimization?

The Lead-to-Customer stage requires the most optimization because this is where the largest loss of potential customers occurs. Improving follow-up strategies and customer engagement can help increase final conversions.


---

Key Insights

A significant number of customers are contacted, but only a fraction convert.

Customer conversion rates vary across communication channels.

Certain months demonstrate stronger conversion performance than others.

Some age groups and job categories contribute more conversions.

Funnel analysis highlights clear opportunities to reduce customer drop-off.



---

**Recommendations**

Prioritize marketing efforts on the highest-converting communication channels.

Improve lead nurturing strategies to convert more engaged customers.

Increase campaign investments during high-performing periods.

Develop targeted campaigns for customer segments with strong conversion potential.

Continuously monitor funnel performance to identify and address bottlenecks.



---

**Conclusion**

The Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis provides valuable insights into customer behavior and campaign effectiveness. By identifying funnel drop-offs, evaluating channel performance, and understanding customer segments, organizations can optimize marketing strategies and improve overall conversion rates.
